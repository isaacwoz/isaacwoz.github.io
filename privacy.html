Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Personal Finance Assistant is a private, non-commercial tool for viewing and analysing the bank accounts of its owner. This page explains how it is intended to handle financial information. It is not a public service or an invitation for other people to connect accounts.

1. Who operates this tool?

The tool is operated by an individual for personal financial management. For questions about the tool or its handling of information, contact: isaac.worrell@icloud.com.

2. What information may be accessed?

With the account holder's authorisation, the tool may use Enable Banking to retrieve available bank account information, including:

  • Bank and account identifiers or details returned by the bank;
  • Account balances and currencies;
  • Transaction dates, amounts, payee or merchant names, references and descriptions;
  • Information needed to maintain an authorised connection, such as session identifiers.

The exact fields depend on the bank and the permissions granted. Bank login credentials are not requested or stored by this website.

3. Why is it used?

Financial information is used for the owner's personal purposes, such as categorising spending, analysing transactions, summarising account activity and preparing personal budgets. The tool is not intended to initiate payments or transfers.

4. How is information obtained?

Account access is arranged through Enable Banking after an authorisation step with the relevant bank. Enable Banking and the bank operate their own systems and have their own privacy notices and terms. Access is limited by the permissions and validity period of each authorised connection.

5. Where is information stored and who might receive it?

Depending on how the owner's script or agent is configured, financial information may be processed in memory or saved in private local files. It is not intentionally published on this GitHub Pages website.

If an externally hosted AI model or service is connected to the agent, selected transaction information may be sent to that provider for analysis. That provider's privacy policy, retention settings and terms will govern its own processing. The provider and the particular data shared will depend on the configuration in use. The owner should review that configuration before enabling transmission.

Financial information is not sold or used for advertising by this tool.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally saved financial data and connection information may remain until the owner deletes them or ends the connection. Deleting local files does not necessarily delete information already processed by Enable Banking, a bank, an AI provider or a backup service; their separate retention policies may apply.

7. Security

Access credentials and private signing keys are intended to remain in private storage, not in the public GitHub Pages repository. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. The owner is responsible for configuring secure storage and restricting access to the script and any AI service it uses.

8. Your choices

The account holder controls which accounts are authorised and can withdraw consent using the relevant bank's tools where supported. Access may also be ended by closing the corresponding Enable Banking session or discontinuing use of the tool. Contact the email above for questions about records maintained by the tool.

9. This website

These pages are static policy documents hosted using GitHub Pages. They do not include application analytics scripts or intentionally set tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical information as the hosting provider under its own privacy statement.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated if the tool's integrations, storage or processing arrangements change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised.
