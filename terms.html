These terms describe Personal Finance Assistant, a private tool created for its owner's personal and non-commercial use. It is not currently offered as a service to the public.

1. Purpose and access

Personal Finance Assistant is intended to retrieve and analyse the owner's authorised bank-account information, including balances and transaction history, to help with personal budgeting and record-keeping. It is not designed for access by other users.

2. Bank connections and consent

The tool uses the Enable Banking API where available. Any account connection requires the relevant account holder's authorisation through the bank or the provider's authorisation process. Banking data access remains subject to the bank's and Enable Banking's terms, permissions, availability and connection-expiry rules.

3. Permitted use

Only bank accounts the operator is authorised to access may be connected. The tool is intended for read-only account information analysis; it is not intended to send money, create payment orders, trade assets or make changes to bank accounts.

4. AI-generated analysis

An AI model may assist in categorising transactions, summarising spending or generating suggestions. AI outputs can be inaccurate, incomplete or based on misclassified transactions. All results should be checked against original bank records before relying on them.

Outputs are for personal information and planning only. They are not regulated financial advice, investment recommendations, tax advice or legal advice.

5. Availability

Connections and results depend on participating banks, Enable Banking, internet access and any configured AI provider. Account data may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. The tool may be modified, paused or discontinued at any time.
